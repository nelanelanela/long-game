Tickle the sun · it giggles

A small secret for anyone who pokes the sun: it squishes, giggles (with a real child’s laugh, if the sound is on), wiggles and winks. About one second, then back to work.

1 · Tap0.00 s

A tap anywhere on the sun (a 44 pt minimum target, even when it’s small).

2 · Squish0–0.08 s

It squashes down like a soft ball and opens its eyes in surprise. Soft haptic.

3 · Giggle0.08–0.7 s

Eyes squeeze shut, mouth opens, cheeks flush, and you hear a little laugh if the sound is on. It wiggles left-right, getting smaller, with two little hops. Rays flick out.

3 · Giggle (other side)

4 · Settle0.7–1.0 s

Back to its happy face with a quick wink, as if sharing the joke. Total about one second.
