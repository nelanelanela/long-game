Tickle the sun · it laughs like a baby

A small secret for anyone who pokes the sun: it gasps, then laughs like a baby: eyes scrunched, head tipped back, bouncing in little hiccups (with a real child’s laugh, if the sound is on). Just over a second, then back to work.

1 · Tap0.00 s

A tap anywhere on the sun (a 44 pt minimum target, even when it’s small).

2 · Gasp0–0.10 s

Like a baby just before a laugh: it stretches up with a little gasp, eyes wide, mouth a round “o”. Soft haptic.

3 · Belly laugh0.10–0.90 s

Eyes scrunch into happy crescents, a big open laugh, round pink cheeks. The head tips back and the whole sun bounces in quick little “ha-ha-ha” hiccups…

3 · …squash, stretch

Five bounces, each a little smaller: squash down, stretch up. Rays jiggle along. The laugh sound plays in the same rhythm.

4 · Contented0.90–1.25 s

It settles with eyes still closed and a happy smile, one last tiny hiccup, and the blush slowly fades.
