A sunrise every time you open it 1 · The launch screen is what iOS shows instantly, before the app has loaded, so it must be still: the night sea, a few faint stars and the sun sleeping on the horizon, half under the water. No text (Apple’s guidance: launch screens aren’t localised and shouldn’t look like an ad). 2 · Sunrise (0–0.6 s, in the app): the sky warms from navy through dusk to morning, the stars fade, the sun rises, its rays unfold and the water turns from night blue to day blue. 3 · Good morning (0.6–0.8 s): the sun opens its eyes, then glides into its place on Home’s Today arc as the screen settles on Home. Short and polite. Only on a cold start, never more than about 1 second, and any tap skips it. When the app returns from the background, it just appears. With Reduce Motion: a 0.3 s cross-fade from the night frame to Home. Water rule as everywhere: the sea is opaque and in front, so the half-risen sun is truly half under water. Dark mode uses the same night frame; the sunrise ends on Home in the current appearance. On iPad and Mac the scene simply widens: the sea always spans the full width.
