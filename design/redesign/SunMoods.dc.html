Weather in the sceneThe weather outside your window passes quietly through Home’s landscape. No costumes: the sun stays itself, and on a clear day nothing changes at all.
CloudSoft clouds of every size drift slowly: big ones high up, small flat ones near the horizon, a few thin wisps.Mostly cloudy or overcast.
RainFine rain, rings on the water, the light a touch cooler. The sun rests its eyes.Rain or drizzle.
WindThe dune lines and the water move faster; a few gusts pass low.Wind of 40 km/h or more.
MistA pale veil lies along the horizon.Fog, mist or haze.
SnowSlow flakes. The sun watches them.Snow or sleet.
The rules
A thin layer. White and pale only, drawn over the sky and sea. The sky’s colour, the sun’s height and its lit rays stay about your progress.The sun is never hidden. Clouds keep to the sides, and its face stays clear. It only changes its eyes: resting in rain, open in snow.Home only. Not in the timer, widgets, the icon, share cards or the Watch.Quiet. Slow movement, no sound. With Reduce Motion the layer is still.One at a time: snow, rain, mist, wind, cloud.
Weather and privacy
Off until you turn it on. Settings › “Weather in the scene”. Turning it on asks for your location, approximate only.From Apple Weather. Asked a few times a day at most. Your location isn’t saved or sent anywhere else.Needs the WeatherKit service switched on in the Apple Developer account, an Apple Weather credit in Settings, and a line in the privacy policy.
