Follows the iPhone by default. Settings › Appearance: Automatic, Light or Dark.The sun still shows progress. In the dark look the sky is always night, so the sun’s height, its glow and the lit arc carry the progress. On an empty day the sun rests on the horizon with no lit arc.The Timer’s sunrise is the same in both looks, but in the dark look it stops at dawn colours instead of a bright day sky, so the screen never glares at night.Calendar colours, the sun and session pictures stay exactly the same.Unchanged: year cards and the milestone card (their look is chosen when sharing), the app icon, the launch screen (already night).Widgets follow the system: the night sky and land in the dark look.Text contrast stays at 4.5:1 or better; the gold button carries navy text.
