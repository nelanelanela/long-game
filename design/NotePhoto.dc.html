Low key, always optional Two quiet fields in the Finish sheet: a dashed photo square and “A note, if you like”. Nothing is pre-selected, nothing is required, and Save looks exactly the same either way. The keyboard and camera never open by themselves. Never asked again. No reminders, no “add a photo?” prompts, no empty-state nagging, no counts of how many sessions have notes. Add or change later from the session’s row in the day (tap the row → the same two fields). That’s also how a session finished from the Live Activity gets its note or photo. One photo per session, from the camera or the library (the library picker needs no permission). Stored on the phone, synced with iCloud only if sync is on, removed when the session is deleted. In History a day with a photo gets a tiny white dot in its calendar square. The day card shows each session’s photo and note. Tapping a photo opens it full screen; swiping moves through earlier photos, so your work in progress becomes a quiet diary. Left out on purpose: session ratings, tags and prompts like “what needs work?”. They’d turn a pause for breath into a form.
