iCloud instead of Sign in with Apple Sign in with Apple is a login: it would give Long Game an account for its own server. Long Game has no server, so it would only add a step. iCloud uses the Apple Account already on the iPhone. The game is stored in the user’s private iCloud (CloudKit), synced to their other devices and restored on a new iPhone. No sign-in screen, no password, and nobody else can read it, not even us. So: drop Sign in with Apple. Step 5 “Keep your game safe” has one switch, on by default; Settings has the same switch. If the iPhone isn’t signed in to iCloud, the card says “Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app to keep a copy” and the switch is off. Welcome back. On first launch, if iCloud already holds a Long Game, Welcome becomes “Welcome back · We found your game in iCloud · Piano · 3,294 hours” with Continue my game / Start a new game.
