No banner on the session screen. When today’s stretch is covered, one quiet gold line appears under the clock and the sun winks once. The session keeps running.

How it works Its own tab. Home gets three tabs: Today, This week, The long game. Each shows one sun, one number and one quiet line. This week and The long game stay exactly as they are. Today’s stretch = your weekly pace ÷ 7, rounded to 5 minutes. 7 h a week is 1 h a day; 12 h a week is 1 h 45. The same every day, no guessing which days you’ll play. The picture. The sun walks the day’s arc from the horizon up towards the moon. Reaching it is today’s stretch done: one more step towards the future you’re playing for. No scale or ticks here: the arc is the scale. Today’s sessions are listed under the line: time, note and photo, with today’s total adding up to the number above. Stretch covered during a session never interrupts it: no pop-up, no sound, no haptic. There is no banner on the session screen: the sun winks once and a quiet gold line appears under the clock. The moment is celebrated on Home (Today tab) and in the Finish sheet afterwards. Never a penalty. A missed stretch isn’t marked anywhere.
