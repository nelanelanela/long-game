Circular: a ring that fills with this week’s hours, the sun inside. Rectangular: hours this week with a bar, and the long-game total. Both follow the Lock Screen tint.

How they update Widgets read the same saved data as the app through a shared App Group. They refresh when a session is saved, at midnight, and on Monday when a new week starts. While a timer runs, the Live Activity takes over; the widgets show the time saved so far. No animation in widgets (iOS doesn’t allow it); the sun is drawn at its current height, with the opaque sea covering its lower part.
