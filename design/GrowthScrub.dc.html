How it works Open. Tap the Growth chart (or its small expand button) and it grows into a full-screen chart, same colours. Turn the phone sideways for a wider view. ✕ or swipe down to close. Animated in. The past line draws from left to right (0.8 s), then the dashed forecast, the likely-range band fades in and the sun pops onto the finish line. Scrub. Touch and drag anywhere on the chart. A thin line and a dot follow your finger along the curve; the headline and a small label show the date and hours. Past points say “Played”, future ones “Forecast” with “If you keep your pace”. Milestones you can feel. Small dots mark 1,000 · 2,500 · 5,000 · 7,500 h (filled when reached, outlined ahead) and the sun at 10,000. Crossing one gives a light haptic tap and a short gold ring; reached ones show their date, future ones the forecast month. Today gives a soft tick too. Follows the slider. If “What if I practise more?” is above 0, the full-screen forecast uses it. Accessibility. Reduce Motion: no draw-in, just a fade. VoiceOver: an Audio Graph of the chart, and swipe up/down to step from milestone to milestone. Haptics only when system haptics are on.
