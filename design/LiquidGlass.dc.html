Rules Glass is for controls only: tabs, toolbar buttons and the main buttons. Content stays flat: the sun and sea, numbers, cards, the calendar, sheets’ contents. No glass on glass. Capsules. Controls become capsules (radius = half the height); cards keep their 20 pt corners. Something to refract. Home and History get a soft warm wash at the top (#FFF0D6 → background) so the glass reads; the hero scrolls under the floating header. Timer. Pause and the close button are clear glass over the night sky; Finish is bright glass. iOS 26 and later only. On iOS 17–25 everything stays exactly as it is now (flat). Uses the system glass, never a home-made blur. Accessibility. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast fall back to solid fills automatically; text stays navy on light glass and white on dark glass, at 4.5:1 or better.
