How the steppers work Tap − or + to move one step: 1 h for years and catch-up weeks, 0.5 h for the weekly target. A light tick with each step. Hold to repeat: after 0.4 s it steps 6 times a second, after 1.5 s 12 times a second. Release to stop. A firmer tick at the limits. Tap the number to type an exact value on a small number pad. Out-of-range values snap to the limit. Quick picks only on the Catch-up average: your usual pace plus a few round numbers. The weekly pace has none. Fill the rest. After changing a year, a text button offers to copy that value to the following years, so a steady habit takes one tap. At a limit (0 or 40) that button greys out; it stays tappable for VoiceOver to say “Minimum”. Buttons 36 pt in rows, 56 pt on big steppers, each with at least a 44 pt touch area. VoiceOver: each row is one adjustable element, “2023, 10 hours a week”; swipe up or down to change. Where: Hours per week each year, the current-year screen, Your pace for the year, both Catch-up screens. The “What if I practise more?” slider stays a slider: it’s for exploring, not choosing.
