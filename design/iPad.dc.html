Portrait · The long game

Home’s three tabs float at the top; a bigger sun; two cards side by side; the two buttons side by side at the bottom.

How the iPad app works One universal app. The iPhone app runs natively on iPad (iPadOS 17+): no separate download, same iCloud game, one session shared with iPhone, Mac and Watch. Layout by window size. Wide windows (landscape, or a big Stage Manager window) get the sidebar layout shared with the Mac: Play (Today, This week, The long game), History (Calendar, Growth, Your 2026), Settings. Tall full-screen windows get the three Home tabs at the top and two cards side by side. Narrow windows (Slide Over, a third of Split View) use the iPhone layout as is. Timer. Landscape puts the sun on the left and the numbers and buttons on the right, sized for practice on a music stand or easel across the room: a 104 pt clock. Keyboard. With a keyboard attached: ⌘N start or finish, Space pause, ⌘L add past hours, ⌘1–⌘5 views. Hold ⌘ to see them all. Also: pointer hover on rows and chart scrubbing; drag a photo from Photos or Files onto a session; Apple Pencil works for the note (Scribble); widgets on the iPad Home Screen and Lock Screen; Live Activity where iPadOS supports it; Liquid Glass on iPadOS 26.
