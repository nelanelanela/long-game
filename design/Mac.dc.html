How the Mac app works Same game, same iCloud. A native SwiftUI Mac app (macOS 14 Sonoma or later) sharing the iPhone app’s models, logic and design system. With Save to iCloud on, sessions, notes and photos appear on both within moments. Free, like the iPhone app, and the same App Store listing (universal purchase). One window with a sidebar: Today, This week, The long game, Calendar, Growth, Your 2026, Settings. The sun gauge on the left, numbers and today’s sessions on the right. Start session and Add past hours in the toolbar. The sun in the menu bar. A small sun shows the running session clock (or today’s time when idle). Its popover starts, pauses and finishes a session without opening the window. Option to hide the Dock icon and keep only the menu bar sun. One session at a time, everywhere. A session started on the Mac shows on the iPhone (and its Live Activity) and the other way round: the running timer’s start and pause times sync through iCloud, and either device can finish it. Keyboard: ⌘N start or finish a session · Space pause or resume · ⌘L add past hours · ⌘1 ⌘2 ⌘3 Today, This week, The long game · ⌘4 ⌘5 Calendar, Growth. Also: the same widgets on the desktop and in Notification Center; notifications from the Mac; drag a photo onto a session to attach it; Liquid Glass on macOS 26.
